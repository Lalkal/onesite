The goal is not to publish random product promotions. Build a focused niche, create useful Pins and use AI to speed up production without sacrificing accuracy or transparency.

01

Create a Pinterest Business Account

Build a focused business profile around a clear niche and organize your content so visitors immediately understand what you offer.

1

Open Pinterest

Go to Pinterest and choose Sign up, or convert an existing account to a Business account.

2

Choose a sign-up method

Use an available option such as Google, email or Apple.

3

Select Business

Choose the business account option to access features available to business creators.

4

Complete your profile

Add your profile name, niche, country and website if applicable.

5

Optimize your bio

Upload a recognizable profile image and write a keyword-relevant description.

6

Create niche boards

Examples include Home Decor, Gadgets, Fitness or Beauty. Keep boards relevant to your audience.

7

Use analytics and claim your site

Enable analytics and claim your website when the relevant Pinterest features are available to you.

02

Register for Amazon Associates

Amazon Associates can allow qualifying traffic sources to earn commissions on qualifying purchases. Requirements and accepted channels vary by country.

1

Use your local Associates program

Visit the Amazon Associates program for the country where you intend to participate.

2

Sign in

Use your Amazon account or create an account as required.

3

Join the program

Follow the current registration process and provide the requested details.

4

Add eligible traffic sources

Provide your website, blog, channel, social profile or other source only where accepted by the applicable program.

5

Describe your traffic strategy

Explain how you plan to attract visitors and create useful content.

6

Complete verification

Provide identity, payment and tax information when requested.

7

Generate approved links

After acceptance, use the affiliate-link tools and methods provided by the applicable Associates program.

Important: Always check the current Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and country-specific requirements. Also check Pinterest's current policies before publishing affiliate content.
03

Create Pinterest Content with ChatGPT

Use ChatGPT as a production assistant for concepts, copy and checklists. Keep final responsibility for product facts, image rights and policy compliance.

1

Choose the product

Review the current product page, features, use cases and limitations.

2

Prepare a visual reference

Use product imagery only when you have appropriate permission or licensing. An online image is not automatically free to reuse.

3

Give ChatGPT context

Explain your audience, niche, desired style and the truthful benefits you want to communicate.

4

Create a Pin concept

Request a clean vertical composition with the product as the hero element and no misleading edits or unsupported claims.

5

Plan the format

A common Pinterest vertical canvas is 1000 × 1500 px. Verify current Pinterest recommendations before publishing.

6

Generate supporting copy

Ask for a title, useful description, relevant keywords and a concise call-to-action.

7

Review before publishing

Check product claims, disclosures, image rights, links and current Pinterest/Amazon requirements.

📌

Pin Design

Use one clear visual idea, strong hierarchy and readable text.

✍️

Copywriting

Write for the searcher's problem and intended use, not only the product name.

🔍

SEO

Use relevant search terms naturally in titles and descriptions.

⚖️

Compliance

Review disclosure, affiliate-link and visual-use requirements before publishing.

04

Example ChatGPT Prompt

This prompt is designed to create a useful Pin concept while emphasizing truthful presentation.

Create a premium Pinterest Pin concept from this product reference.

Goal: Help shoppers understand the product without misleading claims.
Style: Clean, modern, premium and minimal.
Composition: Vertical Pinterest-friendly layout, approximately 1000 × 1500 px.
Visual direction: Clean background, balanced lighting, realistic shadows and clear product hierarchy.
Product: Keep the product as the hero element. Do not invent features, alter important product characteristics or imitate copyrighted branding beyond what is necessary and permitted.

Also generate:
1. An SEO-optimized Pinterest title
2. A useful Pinterest description
3. 15 relevant Pinterest keywords/tags
4. A short, non-misleading call-to-action
5. A checklist of product claims or details I should verify before publishing
Fact-check:

Review AI-generated product details against the current product page. AI output should not be treated as proof of price, availability, specifications or performance.

05

Best Practices

The strongest affiliate content is useful first and promotional second. Trust is an asset, so keep recommendations accurate and transparent.

📜

Follow Program Rules

Read the current Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and Pinterest policies applicable to your account and region.

🏷️

Disclose Affiliate Relationships

Use clear disclosures wherever required and do not hide the commercial relationship.

🖼️

Use Licensed Visuals

Prefer original visuals or assets you have permission to use.

💎

Make Helpful Pins

Focus on use cases, comparisons, buying considerations and problem-solving rather than exaggerated promotions.

Quick Publishing Checklist

  • My Pinterest profile clearly explains the niche.
  • The Pin gives the viewer a useful reason to click.
  • Product claims have been verified.
  • I have permission or a valid license for visual assets.
  • My affiliate disclosure is clear where required.
  • The destination link is correct and permitted.
  • Titles and descriptions use relevant keywords naturally.
  • The Pin makes no misleading product promises.
  • I reviewed the current Amazon Associates and Pinterest requirements.
