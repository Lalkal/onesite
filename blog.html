Article 01 • Business

Top 10 Business Ideas: Basics, Rules & Income Potential

A practical overview of business models and the basic operating principles highlighted in the Get Idea with LoganX guide.

1. Personal Branding — 10/10

Build an audience around your knowledge and personality using platforms such as Instagram, YouTube or LinkedIn. The guide presents this as a high-potential model with a broad income range.

Basic rule: Pick one niche and platform, publish consistently, give value first and build trust before monetising.

2. Airbnb Rental Arbitrage — 10/10

Rent a property with permission, furnish it and operate it as a short-term rental where legally and contractually permitted.

Basic rule: Get written permission, price dynamically, respond quickly and create a reliable cleaning/caretaking process.

3. AI Automation — 8.5/10

Help businesses automate repetitive work such as content production, customer service, lead generation, email and data analysis.

Basic rule: Master a few tools, choose one industry, create a case study and charge for measurable value.

4. Digital Marketing — 8/10

Offer services such as social media, advertising, SEO and content strategy to businesses.

Basic rule: Learn one skill deeply, demonstrate results and move toward recurring retainers.

5. Turf Business — 7/10

Operate artificial turf, badminton or similar sports facilities and rent them by the hour.

Basic rule: Location is critical. Add online booking, strong maintenance and corporate/school partnerships.

6. Real Estate — 7/10

Participate in property sales, rentals or brokerage, depending on your capital, skills and local rules.

Basic rule: Learn the local market, build relationships and follow applicable registration and legal requirements.

7. Cloud Kitchen — 7/10

Run a delivery-focused food operation without the same dine-in footprint as a restaurant.

Basic rule: Start with one strong cuisine, control packaging and ratings, and locate close to your delivery demand.

8. Franchise Business — 6/10

Operate under an established brand and system.

Basic rule: Research the brand, royalties, location economics and franchise-owner experiences before committing.

9. Tea Shop — 5/10

A simple tea and snack business can work when location, consistency and speed are strong.

Basic rule: Prioritise footfall, consistent taste, a simple menu and high-demand time periods.

10. Stock Market — 5/10

The guide lists stock-market participation as variable and skill dependent, with substantial risk.

Basic rule: Use only money you can afford to invest, learn before acting, diversify and avoid relying on tips.

Final takeaway

The strongest theme across the list is execution. A business model is only an opportunity until you validate demand, build a repeatable process and consistently serve customers.

Article 02 • Affiliate Marketing

Pinterest + Amazon Affiliate + ChatGPT Workflow

A practical content workflow using Pinterest for discovery, affiliate marketing for monetisation and ChatGPT as a production assistant.

Step 1: Build your Pinterest presence

Create or convert to a Pinterest Business account, choose a clear niche, complete your profile and publish useful content consistently.

Step 2: Choose a product category

Select products that fit your audience. Review the current product information and make sure anything you say about the product is accurate.

Step 3: Set up the affiliate relationship

Apply to the relevant Amazon Associates programme and follow the current requirements for your country and account. Complete verification and use only approved affiliate-link methods.

Important: Amazon Associates and Pinterest requirements can change. Always review the current programme agreements and platform policies before publishing.

Step 4: Create a Pin with ChatGPT

  1. Choose and verify the product.
  2. Prepare a visual reference that you have permission to use.
  3. Give ChatGPT the audience, niche, style and truthful product benefits.
  4. Ask for a clean vertical Pin concept.
  5. Generate a useful title, description, keywords and CTA.
  6. Review all claims, links, disclosures and image rights before publishing.

Copy-ready ChatGPT prompt

Create a premium Pinterest Pin concept from this product reference. Goal: Help shoppers understand the product without misleading claims. Style: Clean, modern, premium and minimal. Composition: Vertical Pinterest-friendly layout, approximately 1000 × 1500 px. Visual direction: Clean background, balanced lighting, realistic shadows and clear product hierarchy. Product: Keep the product as the hero element. Do not invent features, alter important product characteristics or make unsupported claims. Also generate: 1. An SEO-optimized Pinterest title 2. A useful Pinterest description 3. 15 relevant Pinterest keywords/tags 4. A short, non-misleading call-to-action 5. A checklist of product claims I should verify before publishing

Best practices

  • Follow current Amazon Associates and Pinterest rules.
  • Clearly disclose affiliate relationships where required.
  • Use original or properly licensed visuals.
  • Make Pins useful first and promotional second.
  • Verify product claims, price and availability against current sources.
Publishing checklist: Verify the product, visual rights, disclosure, destination link, keywords and all claims before publishing.
Article 03 • Creator Guide

Budget-Friendly Content Creation Plan

You do not need a cinema camera or expensive studio. Start with what you own and improve the things viewers notice most.

1. Budget-friendly gadgets

Your smartphone can produce excellent footage. The guide recommends prioritising audio before expensive camera upgrades.

  • Camera: use your current smartphone and clean the lens.
  • Microphone: a basic lavalier can significantly improve voice clarity.
  • Stability: use a simple tripod or ring-light stand with a phone mount.
  • Upgrade order: audio → lighting → stability → camera.

2. Low-cost lighting

Natural light from a large window can work extremely well. For night shoots, use a ring light or affordable LED panel.

  • Use a window or lamp as the key light.
  • Use white foam board as inexpensive fill.
  • Use a small background light for separation.
  • Keep the face brighter than the background.
Budget tip: Experiment with your nearest large window before buying lights. Placement often matters more than price.

3. Creative backdrops

A professional background does not have to be expensive. A tidy room corner, bookshelf, plant and small lamp can create an intentional look.

  • Use paper or non-reflective fabric.
  • Consider one accent wall.
  • Remove cables and clutter.
  • Keep the background consistent with your content.

4. Professional, low-cost editing

Use an editor that matches your skill level and publishing speed. The guide highlights DaVinci Resolve, CapCut and Photopea for different needs.

5. Content idea generation

Do not depend entirely on inspiration. Use search behaviour and a repeatable content pipeline.

  • Use question/topic research to find audience problems.
  • Use Google Trends to compare interest and discover related topics.
  • Turn one “hub” idea into several smaller “spoke” pieces.
Content system: One long-form idea can become a blog post, YouTube video, short clips, carousel posts and multiple social captions.

6. Hooks and captions

Start with a clear problem, promise or curiosity gap.

Example hooks:

“Stop wasting money on expensive cameras. Do this instead.”

“Here is the exact 3-step blueprint I use to light videos on a budget.”

“Your videos look flat because of one lighting mistake. Here's how to fix it.”

Quick creator checklist

  • Clean your phone camera lens.
  • Test your microphone.
  • Face a window or set your key light.
  • Remove background distractions.
  • Record a short test clip.
  • Edit with a simple workflow.
  • Write a strong first line.
  • Publish consistently and review performance.
